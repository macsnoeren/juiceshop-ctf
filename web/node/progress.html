Using curl, sqlmap or another tool outside the browser

The shop only lets you in with your door cookie. Burp and ZAP work through your browser and carry it automatically. A command-line tool needs it passed in:

curl -b '{{cookieName}}={{.Cookie}}' {{if .Secure}}https{{else}}http{{end}}://{{.Host}}{{.JuiceShopPath}}rest/products/search?q=apple
sqlmap -u '{{if .Secure}}https{{else}}http{{end}}://{{.Host}}{{.JuiceShopPath}}rest/products/search?q=apple' --cookie '{{cookieName}}={{.Cookie}}'

Treat this value like a password: anyone who has it can use your shop.
